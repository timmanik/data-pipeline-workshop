About This Dataset

The NOAA Global Surface Summary of Day (GSOD) provides comprehensive daily weather summaries from thousands of weather stations worldwide.

Data Coverage

This database contains weather data from 2020 to present, compiled from over {{ summary_stats.location_count }} global weather stations. The data includes observations from the USAF Climatology Center and NCDC.

Update Schedule

The dataset is periodically updated with new weather observations. Each record represents a daily summary derived from multiple hourly observations throughout the day.

Available Weather Measurements

Each weather station can report up to 18 different meteorological elements:

  • Mean temperature (0.1 Fahrenheit precision)
  • Mean dew point (0.1 Fahrenheit precision)
  • Mean sea level pressure (0.1 mb precision)
  • Mean station pressure (0.1 mb precision)
  • Mean visibility (0.1 miles precision)
  • Mean wind speed (0.1 knots precision)
  • Maximum sustained wind speed
  • Maximum wind gust
  • Maximum temperature
  • Minimum temperature
  • Precipitation amount (0.01 inches precision)
  • Snow depth (0.1 inches precision)
  • Fog occurrence indicator
  • Rain or drizzle indicator
  • Snow or ice pellets indicator
  • Hail indicator
  • Thunder indicator
  • Tornado/Funnel cloud indicator

Common Use Cases

Climate Research

Analyze long-term weather patterns, study climate variations, and identify trends across different geographical regions.

Agricultural Planning

Support farming decisions with historical weather data for crop planning, irrigation scheduling, and risk assessment.

Machine Learning

Train predictive models for weather forecasting, anomaly detection, and environmental impact studies.

Data Quality Information

Important Notes

• Daily summaries require a minimum of 4 observations per day to ensure data quality

• All times are based on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT, 0000Z - 2359Z)

• Some values may show slight rounding due to unit conversions

• Visibility data may cluster around certain values due to reporting practices

• Extreme values (wind gusts, precipitation) appear only when reported by stations
